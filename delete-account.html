Delete your Pixel Obby account

This page lets you delete a Pixel Obby cloud-save account and its associated cloud data without reinstalling the Android app.

What will be deleted

  • Your Pixel Obby authentication account.
  • Your cloud-saved game progress.
  • Your cloud obby run history associated with that account.

What is not deleted automatically

  • Guest/local progress stored only on your device. You can remove it by clearing Pixel Obby browser/app storage.
  • Anonymous product analytics that are not linked to your account or email address. These may remain in de-identified form for product measurement.

1. Sign in to verify the account

Enter the same parent-managed email used for Pixel Obby Save Online. We will send a passwordless sign-in link.

2. Delete the account

Signed in as

This action permanently deletes the Pixel Obby cloud account and associated cloud data listed above.

If you are a parent or guardian managing a child’s sign-in email, use that same email address here. For other privacy questions, see the Pixel Obby Privacy Policy.
